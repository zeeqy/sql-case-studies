# Part B: business questions (DompetKu e-wallet)

All questions use the synthetic dataset described in [DATA.md](DATA.md). Each one has its own file in [queries/](queries/), and saved outputs are in [results/](results/).

| # | Area | Question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Growth | How many monthly active users do we have, how fast is that growing, and how sticky is the app (DAU/MAU)? |
| 2 | Growth | How many people sign up each month, and what share complete full KYC? |
| 3 | Retention | Of each month's new users, what % are still active 1, 2, 3, 6, 9 and 12 months later? |
| 4 | Retention | Who has churned (no activity in 60+ days), by KYC level and bill-pay use? |
| 5 | Customers | How do users split into RFM segments, and how much spend comes from each? |
| 6 | Merchants | How has each category's share of spend shifted from Q1 2024 to Q4 2025? |
| 7 | Merchants | Which categories grew fastest in 2025, and was it more transactions or bigger tickets? |
| 8 | Merchants | Who are the top 3 merchants in each state, and how concentrated is each state? |
| 9 | Campaigns | Did each cashback campaign actually change behaviour, and by how much? |
| 10 | Campaigns | What did each campaign cost per RM100 of extra spend, and was it worth it? |
| 11 | Risk | Which wallets look like money mules? |
| 12 | Risk | How common are round amounts in normal P2P, so is "round" alone a useful signal? |
| 13 | Activation | How many signups top up, make a first payment, and come back the next month? |
| 14 | Payments | How do people load money (FPX, card, cash counter), and does it differ by state? |
| 15 | Payments | When during the week and day do people pay? |
| 16 | Operations | What are the failure rates by transaction type and channel each quarter? |
| 17 | Customers | Are users who pay bills in the app more valuable? |
| 18 | Network | How reciprocal is the P2P network, and how much money crosses state lines? |
