# Case 1: Danny's Diner: questions

Source: [8 Week SQL Challenge, Case Study #1](https://8weeksqlchallenge.com/case-study-1/) by Danny Ma.
The questions are reworded here. See the original page for the full brief.

A small Japanese restaurant (sushi, curry, ramen) wants to understand its customers and decide whether to expand its loyalty programme.

1. How much has each customer spent in total?
2. On how many separate days has each customer visited?
3. What was each customer's first purchase?
4. Which menu item sells the most, and how many times has it been bought?
5. What is each customer's favourite item?
6. What did each member buy first after joining?
7. What did each member buy last before joining?
8. Before joining, how many items did each member buy and how much did they spend?
9. With 10 points per $1 and double points on sushi, how many points does each customer have?
10. If members also earn double points on everything in their first week (join day included), how many points do A and B have at the end of January?
11. Bonus: build one joined table with a member Y/N flag, and rank purchases only once a customer is a member.
